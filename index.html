ABOUT VARAHI TEXTILES

Built around quality.
Made for institutions.

Varahi Textiles is a Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu based manufacturer and supplier of export fabrics, uniforms and related textile accessories.

We provide textile and uniform solutions for schools, corporate organizations, institutions, colleges and other organizational requirements. Our focus is on supplying practical, dependable and professionally made textile products.

Based in Tiruppur, one of India's major textile manufacturing centres, Varahi Textiles operates with a focus on quality, consistency and reliable supply.

From fabrics and finished uniforms to related accessories, we support organizations with products suited to their specific requirements.
